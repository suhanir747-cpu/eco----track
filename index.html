01 / INTRODUCTION

Understanding Global Warming

Global warming refers to the long-term increase in Earth's average surface temperature, mainly caused by greenhouse gas emissions from human activities.

🌡️

Rising Temperature

Increasing greenhouse gases trap additional heat in the atmosphere and contribute to rising global temperatures.

🏭

Human Activities

Fossil fuel consumption, industrial activities and deforestation contribute to greenhouse gas emissions.

🌊

Environmental Impact

Global warming affects oceans, glaciers, ecosystems, weather patterns and biodiversity.

04 / DIGITAL TECHNOLOGY

Technology Behind Climate Tracking

Modern technologies transform environmental observations into meaningful climate insights.

🛰️

Remote Sensing

Collects information about Earth without direct physical contact using satellites and other instruments.

🌐

Internet of Things

Connected sensors transmit environmental observations to digital platforms.

☁️

Cloud Computing

Supports the storage, processing and sharing of large environmental datasets.

📊

Big Data Analytics

Helps researchers study large datasets and discover long-term environmental patterns.

🧠

Machine Learning

Analyses historical observations to identify patterns and support climate modelling.

📍

GPS Technology

Provides location information useful for environmental surveys and field monitoring.

06 / ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT

Effects of Global Warming

Climate change influences natural systems, communities and ecosystems around the world.

Forest Ecosystems

Changes in temperature and rainfall can affect forests and biodiversity.

Rising Sea Levels

Ocean warming and melting land ice contribute to rising sea levels.

Changing Ecosystems

Climate conditions can influence habitats and species distribution.
